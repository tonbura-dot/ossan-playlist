hint-bar-uploader

A private upload tool for the YouTube channel ヒント屋のヒント。

What it does

hint-bar-uploader is a command-line script used only by the channel owner. It uploads videos that the owner produced, together with the prepared title, description, tags and thumbnail, to the owner's own YouTube channel.

  1. Upload the finished video as private (videos.insert) and set its thumbnail (thumbnails.set).
  2. The owner reviews the private video in YouTube Studio.
  3. Only after the owner approves it, the video is made public or scheduled (videos.update).

It also reads the owner's own channel info (channels.list, mine=true) to confirm it is connected to the right channel. It never accesses other users' data and is not offered to anyone else.

Sign-in

The owner signs in once with Google (OAuth 2.0). Access can be revoked at any time in Google account permissions.
